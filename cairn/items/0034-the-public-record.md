The subway's own track record, measured and shareable.

The poller has recorded the feeds for months and already compares each line's headways with its
own normal. This milestone publishes that as a weekly report card per line, makes any moment of
history a link, and gives researchers a stable, documented way in. It is what earns bound a
voice: journalists, advocates and riders citing what actually happened rather than what was
scheduled.

Ships when a weekly report for every line is published at a stable URL with its method stated,
and a replay of any recorded moment can be shared as a link.
