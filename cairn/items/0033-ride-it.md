The app for the train you are on, not the one you are waiting for.

Follow mode already keeps the screen awake and tracks one train. This milestone makes it an
in-car display: the next stop, the changes there, a warning before your stop — and keeps the
train moving when the signal drops in a tunnel, which an app that only relays the feed cannot
do and a simulation can.

Ships when a rider can follow a train with a destination, be told before their stop, and keep a
moving, honestly-labelled position through a stretch with no connection.
