The answer a commuter opens the app for, before they ask it: which train to leave for, and when.

bound already knows three things no other New York app holds together: where every train
actually is, how sure it is about each arrival (the ±1σ ranges are calibrated: about 68 % of
arrivals land inside them), and how long the walk to the platform takes. This milestone turns
those into one line on the home view — "Leave in 3 min for the 8:42 L" — and then into a push
when that answer changes.

Ships when a rider with a saved station and a usual direction sees a leave-by line on home that
names a train they can catch, checked against recorded history.
