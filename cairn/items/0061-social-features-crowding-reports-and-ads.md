## Decision

Not built, by choice.

bound's claim is "this is true, and this is how sure we are". Each of these weakens it:

- **Rider-reported crowding and delays** are unverifiable, and they would sit beside measured
  data as if they were the same kind of thing.
- **A social layer** (sharing commutes, following other riders) turns a tool into a feed.
- **Ads** make the interface argue with its own answer for attention.

What bound reports, it measures, and what it cannot measure it does not show. Recorded in
README.md under "What bound is" (0036).
